Hello, I'm Zach.

I use smart microscopy, machine learning, and biophysical principles to investigate the structure of subcellular membranes at a resolution below 10 nm.

I am a postdoctoral researcher in the Ries group at the University of Vienna, Max Perutz Labs. Before this, I developed tools for light sheet microscopy at the University of Texas Southwestern Medical Center. I received my PhD in Biomedical Engineering from Yale University.
